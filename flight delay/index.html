Level 1 DFD: End-to-End Prediction Data Flow (Figure 2.3)

1.0

Input Collection (Web Interface)

User selects operating airline, origin, destination, travel date, time, distance, and duration.

2.0

Input Validation (Python Checks)

Validates non-empty fields, asserts origin != destination, checks positive ranges, and sanitizes strings to uppercase.

3.0

Feature Construction & Preprocessing (ColumnTransformer)

Extracts MONTH, DAY_OF_WEEK, SCHEDULED_DEP_HOUR; runs StandardScaler and OneHotEncoder pipelines.

4.0

Model Inference (Random Forest Pipeline)

Queries predict_proba() on the 200-tree ensemble to extract the class 1 (Delayed) probability.

5.0

Risk Categorization & Result Presentation

Translates probability into Low (<30%), Medium (30–60%), High (≥60%) risk with summary table.

Executive Summary

Few services depend on punctuality as much as air travel does. When one flight leaves late, the effect cascades to onward connections, crew duty allowances, and gate congestion. This project develops an end-to-end Machine Learning web application that estimates the probability of scheduled flights arriving at least 15 minutes late (BTS / DGCA Aviation standard). By training on historical flight records with strict isolation of pre-departure features, the system prevents data leakage and eliminates training-serving skew through a serialized Scikit-learn Pipeline.
