# Vercel Deployment Guide for Flight Delay Prediction

Your project is completely ready for **Vercel deployment** with zero-configuration!

---

### Method 1: Deploy in 30 Seconds via Vercel Dashboard (Easiest - No CLI Needed)

1. Open your browser and go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com).
2. Log in with your **GitHub** or **Email** account.
3. Click on the **"Add New..."** button (top right) and choose **"Project"**.
4. You will see an option to **"Drop a folder here to deploy"** or upload your GitHub repo:
   - Either drag and drop the `flight delay` folder from your desktop (`c:\Users\mukes\Desktop\flight delay`).
   - Or push this folder to your GitHub account and click **Import**.
5. Project Name: `flight-delay-prediction`
6. Framework Preset: **Other** (Root Directory: `./`)
7. Click **Deploy**!
8. Within **15 to 20 seconds**, Vercel will give you a live production URL like:
   `https://flight-delay-prediction-xxxx.vercel.app`

---

### Method 2: Deploy via Netlify Drop (Instant Alternative - 10 Seconds)

1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Simply **drag & drop** your `flight delay` folder into the drop zone.
3. It will instantly publish online with a live public HTTPS URL!
